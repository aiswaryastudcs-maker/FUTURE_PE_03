# AI SEO Content Generation Prompts

## Prompt 1: SEO Blog Topic Generator

Act as an SEO content strategist.

Generate 10 blog topic ideas for a selected business or niche.

For each topic, provide:
- SEO-friendly blog title
- Target audience
- Primary keyword
- Search intent
- Suggested content type

Avoid misleading titles and unsupported claims.

## Prompt 2: Keyword Research

Act as an SEO keyword research assistant.

For the selected topic, suggest:
- One primary keyword
- Five secondary keywords
- Five long-tail keyword ideas
- Related questions readers may ask
- Search intent for each keyword

These are keyword ideas, not verified search-volume or ranking data.

## Prompt 3: SEO Blog Outline

Create a detailed SEO-friendly blog outline for the selected topic.

Include:
- H1 title
- Introduction
- H2 headings
- Relevant H3 subheadings
- Frequently asked questions
- Conclusion
- Suggested call to action

Use a logical structure and avoid keyword stuffing.

## Prompt 4: Content Cluster Generator

Act as a content strategist.

Create one pillar page topic and eight related supporting blog topics.

For each supporting topic, provide:
- Suggested title
- Primary keyword
- Search intent
- Suggested link to the pillar page

Explain how the articles can be connected through internal links.

## Prompt 5: Local SEO Content Generator

Act as a local SEO content writer.

Generate five blog topics for a local business.

Ask for the business type and location before generating location-specific content.

Include:
- Blog title
- Local keyword idea
- Target audience
- Suggested call to action

Do not invent business addresses, reviews, or claims.

## Prompt 6: SEO Content Review

Review the supplied blog draft.

Check:
- Title clarity
- Heading structure
- Keyword relevance
- Readability
- Search intent
- Internal linking opportunities
- Unsupported claims

Provide specific improvement suggestions without guaranteeing search rankings.
