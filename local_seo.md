# Local SEO Content Generator

## 1. Objective

Generate useful, search-friendly website content for a local business using its service, target city, audience, and business details.

## 2. Input Details

- Business Name: [Enter Business Name]
- Business Type: Digital Marketing Agency
- Target City: [Enter City]
- Main Service: Digital Marketing Services
- Target Audience: Small Business Owners
- Primary Keyword: Digital Marketing Services in [City]
- Secondary Keywords:
  - Digital Marketing Agency in [City]
  - SEO Services in [City]
  - Social Media Marketing in [City]
- Tone: Professional, friendly, and informative
- Call to Action: Contact us to discuss your marketing needs.

## 3. AI Prompt

Act as an experienced local SEO content writer.

Create an SEO-friendly landing page for a digital marketing agency serving [City].

Use the following business information:
- Business name: [Enter Business Name]
- Main service: Digital Marketing Services
- Target audience: Small Business Owners
- Primary keyword: Digital Marketing Services in [City]

Requirements:
1. Write an SEO title that naturally includes the primary keyword.
2. Write a concise meta description.
3. Create an H1 heading.
4. Write an introduction explaining the service.
5. Add H2 and H3 headings covering the services and their benefits.
6. Explain how the services can help small businesses.
7. Include a useful FAQ section.
8. Add a clear call to action.
9. Use keywords naturally without keyword stuffing.
10. Do not invent customer reviews, awards, prices, statistics, addresses, or business achievements.
11. Do not claim that the business is located in a city unless that information is verified.
12. Use placeholders when business details are missing.

Return the landing page in Markdown format.
